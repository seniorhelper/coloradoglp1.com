What this site is

A gap between a prescription
and good information.

GLP-1 medicines get a lot of attention, and a lot of the information online is written to sell something. This site exists to give Colorado readers a calm, neutral starting point before they talk to a clinician.

It covers the basics: what GLP-1 medicines are, which brands are FDA-approved, how they work, the most common side effects, how cost and insurance usually work, and how to tell a licensed prescriber and pharmacy from a risky online seller.

Medication works best alongside a real lifestyle plan — nutrition, activity, sleep and follow-up care — not as a replacement for one. A licensed clinician who knows your health history is the right person to decide whether any of it fits you.

coloradoglp1.com does not prescribe, sell or ship medication, and it is not affiliated with any clinic, pharmacy or manufacturer.

How the content is written

Four principles.

The same standards a reader should expect from any health information they find online.

FDA

Approved medicines only

The guide focuses on FDA-approved GLP-1 brands: semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy, Rybelsus) and tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound).

YOU

Reader-first

No products, no quizzes and no sign-up funnels. The goal is to help you ask better questions of your own clinician.

SCI

Evidence-based

Explanations follow the published prescribing information and peer-reviewed research, not trends or social media claims.

MD

Not medical advice

Nothing here replaces an evaluation by a licensed clinician who can review your history, other medicines and goals.
